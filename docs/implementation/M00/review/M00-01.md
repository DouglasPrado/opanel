## Findings

### F-1 — RSpec introduced in M00-01 rather than in M00-07

- **Dimensão:** Escopo
- **Severidade:** Low
- **Evidência:** `Gemfile:26`, `spec/spec_helper.rb`, `spec/rails_helper.rb` — the
  test runner arrives one Story before the harness Story that configures it.
- **Ação:** accepted deliberately and recorded in the Story Report. M00-01
  declares two required tests and SC-03 already froze RSpec; writing them in
  Minitest and rewriting them in M00-07 would widen the diff, not narrow it.
  `spec/rails_helper.rb` stays deliberately free of database configuration so
  M00-02 and M00-07 keep their full scope.
- **Estado:** closed

### F-2 — Deleting `config/master.key` changes how production boots

- **Dimensão:** Segurança
- **Evidência:** `config/credentials.yml.enc` and `config/master.key` removed; the
  production environment resolves `secret_key_base` from credentials by default.
- **Severidade:** Low
- **Ação:** intentional. Keeping an unused master key in an autonomous workspace
  contradicts Annex H §3.2, and the frozen decision is that configuration comes
  from the environment. The consequence — `SECRET_KEY_BASE` becomes a required
  key whose absence must abort the boot — is exactly M00-16's acceptance
  criterion 2, and is recorded as a pendency in the Story Report so it cannot be
  forgotten. Development and test are unaffected: Rails generates a local secret.
- **Estado:** closed

### F-3 — Development boot returns 500 until M00-02

- **Dimensão:** Operação
- **Severidade:** Low
- **Evidência:** `log/development.log` —
  `ActiveRecord::Migration::CheckPending#call` cannot reach `opanel_development`.
- **Ação:** not a defect. `M00-02 dependsOn M00-01` in `tasks.json`; the database
  is the next Story. The Story's integration criterion is proven in the test
  environment, where the middleware is absent. Recorded as a pendency.
- **Estado:** closed
