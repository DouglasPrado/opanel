## Findings

### F-1 — The secret scan's reach into the pack is now asserted, not assumed

- **Dimensão:** Segurança
- **Severidade:** Medium (closed by this Story)
- **Evidência:** a planted GitHub token in `docs/implementation/M00/reports/` is
  detected; the allowlist is asserted not to mention `docs/implementation`,
  `reports` or `evidence`.
- **Ação:** this was the gap worth closing. A Story Report is exactly where a
  credential arrives by accident — someone pastes the output of a command that
  printed one — and until now the coverage was a property of the allowlist
  happening not to exclude it, which nothing was watching.

### F-2 — The templates predate this commit

- **Dimensão:** Escopo
- **Severidade:** Low
- **Evidência:** `docs/templates/*.md` and most of
  `spec/documentation/templates_spec.rb` were written earlier in the Milestone,
  before the gates that consume them existed.
- **Ação:** accepted. The Story is closed by the two things that were genuinely
  missing — AC9 and AC10 — and the report says so rather than claiming the whole
  surface as new work. Nothing about the templates changed here.
